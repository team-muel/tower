# T56 — Generated encounter combat runtime

**Branch** `task/T56-combat-view-integration` · **PR title** `T56: connect generated encounters to combat runtime`

## Purpose

Connect T54's generated floor encounter host to the T55 real-time combat
driver and replace the temporary manual resolve seam with combat-state victory.

## Contract

- The player and all three companions use their shared authored humanoid
  presentation while retaining distinct character definitions and unit ids.
- Entering a scheduled encounter holds local movement for the configured intro
  delay, then restores direct player movement during real-time combat.
- The player is externally positioned: autonomous scoring may attack from its
  current world position but may not move it. Companion and enemy movement is
  driven by Core and projected back to their world transforms.
- Generated enemy kind slots resolve through data-authored combat profiles.
  Each living combatant presents Core-authoritative HP and hides on death.
- Player-team victory resolves the generated Run event and unlocks traversal;
  enemy-team victory remains blocked for the Run lifecycle task.
- `-qaAutoEncounter` is a build-only verification entry seam. It places the
  whole party at the scheduled encounter but does not bypass detection, intro
  hold, combat, victory, or traversal resolution.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Player orders, rewards, defeat/retreat, multi-event Run lifecycle, authored
  enemy variety, and final camera/UI polish. These remain downstream tasks.
